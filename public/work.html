The context

Morsa is an AI operations platform serving manufacturers. Bringing that product to market means understanding the workflows and operational pain points of the people who run those businesses.

My contribution

I’m building the North American go-to-market function from the ground up. I designed outbound infrastructure across Attio, Lemlist, Clay, email, and LinkedIn, covering account sourcing, enrichment, deduplication, sequencing, and pipeline management.

I own ideal customer profile development, messaging, prospect research, discovery, and early-stage pipeline generation. I also turn customer conversations into structured product feedback for the founding team.

The work in progress

The focus is a repeatable commercial process: relevant accounts, informed conversations, clear next steps, and useful feedback to the product team. This is an ongoing role; the work is still being built.
