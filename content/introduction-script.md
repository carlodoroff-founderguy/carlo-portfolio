# One-minute personal introduction

Approximately 130 words. Speak naturally and pause briefly between paragraphs. Record one timed take and adjust your pace to finish close to 60 seconds.

Hey, I’m Carlo Doroff. I’m an entrepreneurship student at the University of Miami, and I like turning ideas into things people actually use.

That’s taken me from selling door to door to founding Craze, an AI platform that helped brands understand Gen Z. I built the product myself, grew the business with a small team, and raised eighty thousand dollars before we were acqui-hired.

Today, I’m the founding go-to-market lead at Morsa, helping bring AI into manufacturing operations.

What connects those experiences is curiosity about people—and the drive to do something with what I learn. I enjoy talking to customers, building the first version, and taking responsibility for what happens next.

Outside work, I’m into bodybuilding, psychology, and learning languages.

If you’re building something useful, I’d love to compare notes.

## Recording

- Film horizontally, with the camera at eye level and your eyes near the top third of the frame.
- Face a window or soft light. Choose a quiet space and an uncluttered background.
- Keep your delivery conversational. Look into the lens and use the paragraphs as talking points.
- Record two or three takes. Choose the clearest, most natural one.
- Send the MP4 so it can be embedded on the About page. Captions must match the final recording.
